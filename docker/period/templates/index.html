Health Tips During Periods

Diet

  • Eat iron-rich foods like leafy greens, lentils, and beans.
  • Include fruits, nuts, and whole grains for energy support.
  • Stay hydrated and reduce excess salt and caffeine.

Exercise

  • Try light walking, yoga, or stretching to ease discomfort.
  • Prioritize rest when you feel fatigued.
  • Consistency in gentle activity can help mood and cramps.

Hygiene

  • Change pads/tampons regularly as recommended.
  • Keep the area clean and dry with gentle products.
  • Wear comfortable, breathable cotton underwear.
